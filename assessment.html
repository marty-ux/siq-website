The Process

Five Phases. About Thirty Days.

The read does the heavy lifting. Humans validate every finding before it ships.

01
Days 1-3

Discovery & Connection

30-minute kickoff call to understand your stack, team structure, GTM motion, and pain points. You authorize read-only OAuth connections to your CRM (HubSpot or Salesforce), intent tools (Bombora, 6sense, G2), conversation intelligence (Gong, Chorus), marketing automation, and sales engagement tools. Standard read-only flow. The same thing your team does to connect any tool to your CRM, and fully revocable at any time.

02
Days 3-7

Data Ingestion & Signal Mapping

Scalient IQ reads and maps your signal data. It traces how data moves (or doesn't) between systems, assesses record completeness, freshness, and duplication, and cross-references contacts against accounts to flag missing buying-committee personas. A consultant doing this manually would take 2 to 3 weeks. The read finishes in days.

03
Days 7-21

Analysis & Pricing

Seven core reads run in parallel: pipeline leakage, deal-stall and forecast fidelity, CRM and data fidelity, buying-committee coverage, objection intelligence, AEO/GEO brand visibility, and stack efficiency. Each leak gets an estimated dollar impact. Human checkpoint: a person reviews every finding before it goes into the report. The read surfaces patterns. We validate and contextualize.

04
Days 21-25

Report Build

The Signal Health Report comes together. A one-page executive summary you can share with leadership. Detailed findings per read area. Signal flow visualizations. Benchmarks against similar mid-market GTM orgs. Priority leaks ranked by dollar impact. The report stands alone, no pitch deck attached.

05
Days 25-30

Readout & Next Steps

60-minute readout with your GTM leadership (VP Sales, VP Marketing, RevOps). Walk through findings, answer questions, discuss what each leak is worth. If you want the read to stay on so the numbers refresh on an always-on cadence, that's the always-on subscription conversation. No pressure. The report has standalone value.
