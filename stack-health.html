Executive Summary: Own vs. Use

One number to bring to the budget meeting: what is owned across the GTM stack, what is actually used, and what is feeding nothing. This is the layer no seat-utilization tool measures, because it scores whether a tool's output reaches a revenue decision, not whether somebody logged in.

2

Signal-to-Spend Composite

Every tool is scored on whether its output reaches a revenue decision. The composite is spend-weighted, so a dormant six-figure platform moves it and a dormant seat licence does not. Governance risk the raw score cannot see is then subtracted.

Renewal Calendar and Notice Deadlines
