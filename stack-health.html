Stack Health Report

1
Executive Summary: Own vs. Use
One number to bring to the budget meeting: what is owned across the GTM stack, what is actually used, and what is feeding nothing. This is the layer no seat-utilization tool measures, because it scores whether a tool's output reaches a revenue decision, not whether somebody logged in.
2
Signal-to-Spend Composite
Every tool is scored on whether its output reaches a revenue decision. The composite is spend-weighted, so a dormant six-figure platform moves it and a dormant seat licence does not. Governance risk the raw score cannot see is then subtracted.
How the number was built.
3
Business-Unit Rollup
One score per unit, spend-weighted, reconciling to the whole-stack composite above.
4
Top Dark Stack Findings
Ranked by annual value. Every finding carries its evidence tier, and nothing is called dark on a client-supplied admin export alone.
5
Contracts and Commitments
What the agreements say you bought, against what the telemetry says you use. Seats contracted versus active, and the outcomes a vendor committed to in writing that never arrived.
Committed outcomes with no evidence of delivery.
6
Renewal Calendar and Notice Deadlines
The renewal date is not the deadline. An auto-renewing agreement locks for another term unless written notice lands a set number of days before, so the date that actually matters is the renewal date minus the notice period in the contract. Those are the dates below.
7
Access and Governance
The lowest access ask of any Scalient IQ lens, and that is deliberate.
8
Path Forward
Dormant spend found today gets cancelled and is gone. Re-running this same analysis in six months finds much less, so the recurring product is not re-finding waste. Business units keep buying tools, and every new agreement arrives with its own auto-renewal clause and its own notice period.
