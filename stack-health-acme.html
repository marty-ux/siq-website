The hero finding: Signal Reach, not seat count
Tool Utilization looks almost fine at 61. Most seats get touched. Signal Reach, at 47, tells the real story: fewer than half of Acme's 94 tools actually write output a rep, a manager, or the board ever sees. A tool with a 90% login rate and a 0% Signal Reach is still dark. That's the gap every seat-utilization tool misses, and it's where most of the $2.4M is hiding.
The hero, now a monitored calendar
Signal Reach is up 32 points, Renewal Coverage is up from 22% to 96%, and the gap between "someone logs in" and "the output reaches a revenue decision" has mostly closed. New dark spend still arrives, faster than 90 days, but it no longer sits unreviewed for a year.
