The Read Before You Scale

What a Signal Health Report and a Demand Health Report would surface right now, before the new platform goes to market at volume.

Paradigm is standing up a full go-to-market motion for the new platform at the same time the sales and marketing team is still forming. Josh is twelve days in and already finding the gaps by hand: a demo request that sat untouched for two days, a form nobody was watching, no shared line yet between an MQL and an SQL.

A read does the same kind of work Josh is already doing, just across the whole stack at once. It surfaces the visible gaps, estimates the potential dollar impact where the data supports it, and repeats on a recurring cadence instead of catching it once.

Two Lenses, One Read
Signal Health Report
Sales

Deal-stage fidelity, buying-committee coverage, and CRM data quality in HubSpot. Most relevant once the SDR and AE team turns the new platform on for SMB and mid-market in January, while you run enterprise and large accounts directly in the meantime.

Demand Health Report
Demand

Whether a demo request or content download gets followed up and how fast, where the two websites and form flows leak against each other, and a point-in-time test of whether Paradigm shows up when an enterprise home-care buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Claude for a vendor like it.

Why Before, Not After

The bogus-email test, the two days of silence, the call to go fix the form: that is the right instinct, run manually, on one channel. A read covers every channel and every stage at once, without anyone having to sit and test it themselves, while the new motion is still small enough to fix cheaply.

Once outbound, paid spend, and a full SDR and AE team turn on in January, every gap here gets more expensive to find and more expensive to fix. A read now sets the baseline everything after it gets measured against.

Read-only access to HubSpot and the rest of your revenue and martech stack is all it takes. Nothing new for a team that is already stretched thin standing up a launch to learn, adopt, or maintain. Nothing installed, nothing written back, and access can be revoked at any time.
