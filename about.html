About Scalient IQ

Built by Operators Who've Carried the Number

Scalient IQ wasn't designed in a research lab. It was designed by someone who has run revenue teams, achieved quotas, debugged broken funnels, and stared at the same fragmented dashboard problem you're staring at right now.

The Founder

Two Decades Carrying the Number

Scalient IQ was founded by a revenue leader with 20+ years building and leading revenue across advertising, digital media, and technology. Our founder has sold and led partnerships with CBS Sportsline, NBC, and major sports and entertainment brands; co-founded a digital transformation consultancy; and worked at the intersection of AdTech, MarTech, enterprise partnerships, sports, gaming, and Web3.

Across every one of those roles, the same problem kept showing up: revenue teams drowning in tools, reports, and sync meetings, yet still losing millions to leaks they could not see or measure. Where is pipeline going cold? Which deals are quietly stalled? Where is the CRM drifting from reality?

Scalient IQ was built to close that gap: an always-on, read-only revenue diagnostic that surfaces the revenue leaks visible in your pipeline, data, deals, and AI-search visibility and estimates the potential dollar impact where the data supports it. Not a vendor pitch. A practitioner's instrument.

When you work with Scalient IQ, you're not buying a static playbook or a generic RevOps audit. You're partnering with someone who can think like a CRO, talk like a CMO, and read a GTM stack like a RevOps lead, then hand your team a living read of exactly where the money is leaking and what closing each gap is worth. Intelligence, not execution. The instrument panel, not the driver.
