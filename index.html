Questions, answered

The read-only diagnostic, in plain terms.

What is Scalient IQ?

Scalient IQ is GTM Signal Intelligence: a read-only revenue diagnostic. It reads the tools you already run across two lenses, demand and pipeline, surfaces the revenue leaks visible in your data with a modeled dollar impact, scores your AI-search visibility (AEO/GEO), and refreshes on an always-on cadence. It is intelligence, not execution.

Does Scalient IQ run plays or send messages for me?

No. Scalient IQ is read-only and never writes, sends, or executes. It surfaces the leak, estimates the potential dollar impact, and hands you the play. Your team runs it. It is the instrument panel, not the driver.

Do I need to install anything or replace my CRM?

No. Scalient IQ connects through standard read-only OAuth to the tools you already run, with nothing to install and nothing to rip out. It is typically connected in under an hour, subject to your admin access and connector availability, and revocable anytime.

How does Scalient IQ help with AEO and GEO?

Scalient IQ scores whether AI search engines and answer engines surface and recommend you, part of the demand lens, so you can see and close AI-search visibility gaps alongside your pipeline leaks.

What is the free GTM Readiness Index?

The GTM Readiness Index is a free five-minute, 12-question self-assessment that scores your revenue engine across four dimensions and shows the top gaps to close, before you connect anything. It is the no-risk on-ramp to a full Signal Review.
