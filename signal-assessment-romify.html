Three Pipelines, One Headline
Finding: Sales, Pilot, and Renewal run as separate pipelines but get read blended, which distorts stall, threading, and win-rate views. Fix: segment every readout by pipeline, the single change that makes the rest of this honest. (Already applied here.)
Key Fields Not Enforced
Finding: 29% of companies have no industry, 30% of contacts no title. Fix: make industry + title required on creation and backfill, this is exactly what unlocks the vertical views you want.
Renewals Have No Motion
Finding: 145 renewal deals sit open all year with no cadence or owner, churn is very low, so the $2.08M book is protected by luck, not process. Fix: a 90/60/30 renewal play with an accountable owner.
Zombie Open Deals
Finding: 63 open Sales deals ($619K) carry close dates already in the past and should have been closed-lost long ago, inflating stall and pipeline numbers. Fix: an automated stage-cleanup sweep with owner approval.
Paid Signal Tools Dormant
Finding: Intent, Visitor ID, and Forecasting are live in HubSpot but unused. Fix: switch them on and route into the plays, no new tools required.
Stage Timing Not Tracked
Finding: no reporting on time-in-stage, so you can't see where deals truly stall. Fix: enable stage-entered tracking (now in the assessment pull) for per-rep stall views.
Demo-to-Pilot Is the Cliff
Finding: 101 deals pile up at Demo done and only 52.7% ever advance, yet piloted deals buy 77% of the time, the bottleneck is the demo-to-pilot handoff, not the pilot. Fix: instrument demo-to-pilot conversion and tag objections at the stage they appear.
